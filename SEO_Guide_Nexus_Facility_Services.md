## Section 3 — Google Search Console Setup

Google Search Console (GSC) is a free tool that tells Google your website exists and lets you monitor your search performance.

1. Go to [search.google.com/search-console](https://search.google.com/search-console)
2. Click **Add Property** and enter `https://www.nexusfacility.au`
3. Verify ownership by adding a DNS TXT record (your domain registrar can help with this)
4. Once verified, go to **Sitemaps** and submit: `https://www.nexusfacility.au/sitemap.xml`
5. Go to **URL Inspection** and request indexing for your homepage

Check GSC monthly to see which search queries are bringing visitors to your site and which pages Google has indexed.
